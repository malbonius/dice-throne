# Dice Throne Playtest 0.21.0

Huntress completes the **Season Two** roster. There are now 16 heroes across Seasons One and Two, selectable in either seat. The update includes her board, dice, both sides of the leaflet, Nyra’s Active and Downed dial artwork, the 32-card deck and optional **Primal Roar!** promo.

## Update from 0.20.0

1. Extract this ZIP over your working 0.20.0 repository or assembled release.
2. Upload the extracted files to the existing GitHub Pages branch. Keep the rest of the repository and the existing `/docs` publish folder.
3. Open the app online and wait for **Playtest 0.21.0** and **Ready for offline play**.
4. Choose **Season Two → Huntress** and start a match.

## Nyra

- Nyra starts Active with 5 Health and has a maximum of 7. If she reaches 0, she becomes Downed. At upkeep, a Downed Nyra with at least 5 Health becomes Active. Otherwise she heals 1 and remains Downed.
- While Active, Nyra adds 2 to Huntress’s attack damage. Huntress can also deal 1 damage to her to reroll one die during her Offensive Roll Phase, once per turn.
- Assign damage after defence, modifiers and prevention. Active Nyra can take an eligible attack in full. Nyra’s Bond allows a split or can be spent earlier for 2 immediate healing.
- Maternal Bond’s defence healing is netted against Nyra’s portion. Damage beyond Nyra’s remaining Health stays assigned to her.
