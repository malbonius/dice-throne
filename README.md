# Dice Throne Playtest 0.19.0

Samurai joins **Season Two** in both player selectors. The update includes his board, both sides of the leaflet, dice, status tokens and unique card artwork. His deck has 32 standard cards and the optional **Kensei!** promo.

## Update from 0.18.0

1. Extract this ZIP over your working 0.18.0 repository or assembled release.
2. Upload the extracted files to the existing GitHub Pages branch. Keep the rest of the repository and the existing `/docs` publish folder.
3. Open the app online and wait for **Playtest 0.19.0** and **Ready for offline play**.
4. Choose **Season Two → Samurai** and start a match.

## Rules covered

- Bushido grants Honor to Samurai if he goes first, and at the end of a turn when he used one or two offensive Roll Attempts. Forced rerolls do not count as extra attempts.
- Spend one Honor for +1 damage or two together for +3. If an attack would take you over the cap, you can spend Honor before collecting the incoming token.
- Shame is consumed by a damaging offensive ability and reduces normal and undefendable attack damage. This reduction also affects Artificer's Heal Bot threshold.
- Back Strike is available once damage from an offensive ability is incoming. The return damage resolves alongside the rest of that roll phase's damage.
