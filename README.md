# Dice Throne Playtest 0.25.0

Pale Lady joins **Outcasts** with her double-sided hero board, Moon Shards, Prey and Bleed, 32-card deck and optional **Apogee of the Moon!** promo.

## Update from 0.24.0

1. Extract this ZIP over your working 0.24.0 repository or assembled release.
2. Upload the changed files to GitHub Pages, keeping the existing `docs` folder.
3. Open the app online until it shows **Playtest 0.25.0** and **Ready for offline play**.
4. Select **Outcasts → Pale Lady** for either seat.

Twilight flips Pale Lady to Werewolf at three Moon Shards during her upkeep. Werewolf normally loses a Shard at the end of her turn and flips back at zero. You can roll for Prey before defence or return to it before resolving damage.

Necromancer starts the game with Unearth covering Black Shroud and the Corpse Dial at zero. In his first upkeep, move Unearth to a different offensive ability and collect its Corpses.
