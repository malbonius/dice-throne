## Black Widow implementation and timing

- Begins with 3 Covert Ops. Cap 3; excluded from generic status removal/transfer lists. Once per global turn, usable in own Main Phases for a free hand upgrade or top-three inspection. If no upgrade is seen, optional revealed search adds a chosen upgrade to hand and shuffles. Otherwise order top cards explicitly from top to bottom.
- Red Room Training allows paid upgrades in Roll Phases, including an opponent's Roll Phase. A declared/activated ability keeps its original base version. Upgrade-dependent additional damage continues to reflect the upgrades in play. Count includes passive and defence upgrades. Five upgrades add 1 to attacks.
- Red Room Training II draws for later upgrade plays and free placements, excluding itself. During a two-upgrade Ultimate batch, a passive newly installed in that batch does not grant a draw for the other simultaneous upgrade. An already installed passive grants both draws. Search selections finish and the deck shuffles before any such draws.
- Covert Ops and Cunning! ordering use `widowTask`; Baton Strike II uses `widowReveal`. These mandatory choices block other card plays until finished. Recon and Ultimate continue via `widowAbility`.
- Time Bombs use `timeBombTimers` (each with stable ID and ticks 1 or 2), plus a synchronised numeric `timeBomb` count for common status logic. Cap 2. New bombs use 1 tick if their inflictor has at least 6 upgrades, otherwise 2. Transfer retains the chosen bomb's countdown. Generic removal synchronises timers; shared removal/transfer and Cleanse permit choosing a particular bomb. Defensive incoming-status prevention removes the newly inflicted bomb, preserving any old bomb.
- Upkeep requires one modifiable roll per remaining bomb. A 6 removes it; otherwise advance. Advancing 1 removes the token and queues one isolated 4-damage event. Each explosion has its own response/prevention window. Both players' bombs are advanced by Infiltrate. Base Infiltrate advances existing bombs before inflicting; Infiltrate II inflicts before advancing, including the new bomb.
- Agility cap 2. Its separate dice context can be modified before resolution, including opponent responses. Success on 1–3 adds one half-prevention counter after flat prevention. Two successes prevent all incoming damage, following leaflet rounding. Supports incoming attack, return, isolated and upkeep damage; no prevention of Ultimate damage.
- Elude! requires an unresolved Agility roll showing 5–6. The die remains alterable until it resolves; the final result determines whether full prevention applies. A resolved Agility roll cannot be reopened.
- Sabotage uses 3 dice, or 4 when upgraded. Batons return damage, Espionage prevents damage; Time Bomb requires 1 Widow normally or 2 when upgraded. Four upgrades permit one additional selected-dice Roll Attempt per defence; Better D! remains separate.
- Subversion! adds 2 plus the number of upgrades put into play by that player during the current turn. Counts reset on the next global turn. Assemble and Smoke Screen! follow the supplied scan.

- Gaining a Ray at the cap offers a `cosmicRayOverflow` choice only if an existing eligible Ray may actually be spent on the current attack. An explicit response window allows the opponent to prevent or avoid the damage. - Photon Punch's Flare trigger checks matching numerical faces, rather than the number of Photon symbols. Barrel Roll resolves each subsidiary die by symbol (Photon +2 damage, Ascend +1 Flare, any Hala Star gains one Ray). Aurora Wave uses the total numerical value of two dice; an earlier Cosmic Ray bonus is retained when that total is resolved.
- Energy Refraction rolls 4 dice, or 5 upgraded. Every Hala Star both returns 1 damage and prevents 1. Every pair of Ascend grants one Flare.
- Shine Bright! permits spending up to 3 available CP even at/near the Flare cap; the token count still caps at 3. Scintillate! discards a selected number of Radiance for 3 prevention each. Refuel!, Cosmic Shield! and Ace Pilot! resolve their own supplied symbol outcomes.

## Doctor Strange 0.34.0

Appended as hero ID 27 in the Marvel set. Module `docs/heroes/doctor-strange.js`; artwork `docs/art-hero-28.js`. No ability upgrades. The shared cards use the existing Marvel artwork automatically.

Local card IDs 19–27 are Spells: Vapors of Valtorr, Winds of Watoomb, Images of Ikonn, Rings of Raggadorr, Chains of Krakkan, Fangs of Farallah, Bolts of Balthakk, Mists of Morpheus, Flames of Faltine. IDs 28–32: Foresight!, Cast Away!, Icy Tendrils!, Shields of Seraphim!, Time After Time!. Optional promo 33: Hosts of Hoggoth!.

Flames (27) begins in `spellBook` without its Prepare effect. A `startingSpell` hook excludes it from the shuffled initial deck before drawing the opening hand. The standard 32 physical cards therefore consist of 31 cards in the draw deck/hand plus the prepared starter. Promo adds one. Spells can be sold/discarded normally, but cannot be played through the ordinary action-card control. The Book's passive prepares one hand Spell per global turn, in either Main Phase, without CP cost. Preparing from an ability or spell does not consume that passive allowance.

`strangeTask` is a serialisable queue of preparation, casting, target, removal, ordering and final-disposition steps. Each Spell resolves completely before the next is selected. Prepared Spells leave the Book when selected to Cast; final disposal happens after nested effects. Flames returns to hand; other Spells go to discard. Spells cast from discard remain there unless their effect moves them. The queue tracks active casts and used selections, so Rings cannot cast itself, and its two targets must be different. An active casting card is not available to prepare/cast again until its current effect is complete.

Spell Prepare effects: Vapors heals a chosen Hero 1; Winds optionally removes a chosen removable status (including a particular Time Bomb); Images does nothing; Rings gains Premonition; Chains inflicts Crimson Bands; Fangs removes 1 opposing CP immediately; Bolts gains 1 CP; Mists grants Déjà Vu to a chosen player; Flames deals 1 isolated damage.

Spell Cast effects: Vapors gains 3 Premonition; Winds prepares from discard; Images adds up to 4 attack damage from hand size; Rings casts up to two different discard Spells; Chains makes an attack undefendable; Fangs adds 3 attack damage; Bolts deals 3 isolated damage; Mists heals a chosen Hero 3; Flames adds 1 attack damage and returns to hand. Isolated Spell damage uses separate prevention/avoidance windows, preserves the parent attack, and is not an Attack Modifier. During an Ultimate, opponents cannot alter/prevent these subsidiary effects. Optional targets and healing are explicit UI choices.

Mystic Bolts has separate small/large straight entries (6/9 before Spell effects). Universal Awareness draws before preparing from discard. Wand's prepare and cast effects resolve in that order before the attack's final damage calculation, following the supplied advanced clarification of its “Then”. Crimson Bands is made visible before these subsequent Spell effects; if Winds removes it, it is not reapplied later. Incoming-status prevention still distinguishes a newly applied token from a pre-existing one.

The Ultimate takes only the top five currently available cards, without reshuffling to fill the five. Up to two Spells may be prepared from those cards; the remaining cards are ordered explicitly top-to-bottom before casting up to two prepared Spells. The Book's existing Spells can also be cast. The queue remains locked against opponent actions throughout the Ultimate.

All Doctor Strange “On [symbol]” results occur once per listed outcome, even if the symbol appears twice. Astral Incursion: +3 for Mandala, Premonition for Tome, Crimson Bands for Mantle, one Cast for Amulet. Cloak rolls two dice: Mandala returns 1, Tome gains one Premonition, Mantle prevents 2, Amulet returns 3. Icy Tendrils and Shields of Seraphim likewise resolve each listed symbol once, with different symbols combining.

Premonition works for any holder. A real, unskipped Income Phase offers normal draw, spend 1 for draw 2, or spend 3 for draw 3. Spending requires returning one of those newly drawn cards (not a pre-existing hand card) to the chosen top or bottom of the deck. CP is gained once. The draw/return choice is serialised in `premonitionTask`. First-turn skipped Income, Concussion and Disarm do not offer it. Completion resumes Main Phase 1 without a second Income.

Déjà Vu works for any holder during their own Offensive Roll Phase before activation, including after an announcement or all attempts. It clears a deferred announcement response, concludes the old Roll Phase, removes appropriate expiring statuses (including Blind, Blinding Light and Crimson Bands), then begins a new Offensive Roll Phase with no Income. Barbed Vine damage, if due, resolves before the restart. The once-per-turn hand preparation allowance is retained.

Crimson Bands is a negative token, cap 1. `bandsTurn` records the affected own Roll Phase. It blocks all card play throughout that phase, including subsidiary choices and its ensuing Defensive Roll Phase. It does not restrict the holder's Main Phase or card responses during the opponent's turn. It expires when the affected Roll Phase ends. Statuses/passives and casting spells remain usable. It blocks Black Widow's non-Ultimate free upgrade placement; Ultimate effects are not reduced by it. Existing save shape is preserved when these mechanics are absent.

All 28 art bundles and offline script entries validated; all 15 Strange card crops/titles/costs inspected. Token images preserve supplied bytes. 4,130 differential checks cover the previous 27 heroes and 1,944 state transitions. ## Loki 0.35.0

Loki is appended as hero ID 28 in Marvel. Module: `docs/heroes/loki.js`; artwork: `docs/art-hero-29.js`. All source artwork comes from the supplied Loki archive. Marvel shared card artwork is selected automatically. Unique card IDs: 19 Mockery II; 20 Antics II; 21 Confuddle II / Liar’s Gifts; 22 Tip the Scale II; 23 Vex II; 24 Vilify II; 25 Double Take II / Made You Look; 26 Kneel to Me II / Bewilder; 27 Loki’n to My Eyes!; 28 How Lovely!; 29 Liar Liar!; 30 Pantaloons on Fire!; 31 Outwit!; 32 Take Backsies!; 33 optional Dash of Mischief!. The three Illusion cards are separate reference assets, excluded from the draw deck. No starting status tokens are granted.

- Illusion is Unique, cap 2, removable and non-transferable. `lokiIllusion` stores arrangement, attacker choice, revealed result and resume stage. The arrangement is visible only in the setup screen; the attacker sees three face-down positions. Shuffle is available when the player does not wish to arrange them. Each spend starts with all three cards. Success means the attacker found Loki: no prevention and the attacker gains a Bag created by the defending Loki. Partial Success adds a half-prevention counter; Fail sets full prevention. Defensive abilities and attack-inflicted statuses still resolve. Flat prevention applies before half prevention; two halves prevent all incoming damage. Cannot be used on Ultimate, pure, return, isolated or upkeep damage.
- Bag of Tricks is Positive, cap 2. `trickBags` stores token ID and original Loki seat; numeric `bagOfTricks` is synchronised for common status logic. Each token is removed before its individual upkeep roll. A 1 loses 1 CP; a 6 gains 2 CP; on 2–5 the original Loki chooses heal 2, gain 1 CP or receive 2 damage. `lokiBag` stores the actual roller separately from the chooser. Transfers preserve token identity/owner. `upkeepBagDamage` and `upkeepBagHeal` accumulate until Upkeep concludes, with normal prevention and simultaneous net healing/damage at the Health cap. Pending Bags cannot be skipped by proceeding to Income.
- Outwit! removes a selected Bag and uses the caster as chooser. On 1–5, choose heal 3, gain 3 CP or receive 3 damage; on 6 gain 4 CP. Its damage is a separate preventable source preserving the parent attack. Nested Outwit rolls retain and resume a parent Bag roll, including after isolated damage. Dice use the roller's Hex and normal legal manipulation for the original phase; Ultimate subsidiary dice stay locked.
- Spellbound is Unique, cap 3, removable and non-transferable. `boundAbilities` stores individual IDs and canonical ability names; numeric `spellbound` stays synchronised. Generic declaration filters covered abilities. Small/large straight entries for one named ability share a binding; independent named upgrade abilities do not. Upgrades retain the binding, Ultimates are ineligible, an Unearth-covered name cannot be newly bound, and at least one Offensive Ability must remain uncovered.
- `spellboundExpires` snapshots tokens when an own Roll Phase begins. Only those tokens clear at that phase's conclusion. A token inflicted by Loki's defence therefore permits the current attack and survives for the attacker's following Roll Phase. Déjà Vu and extra Roll Phases respect normal expiry. Actual Cursed Pirate/Pale Lady board flips clear bindings, following the supplied advanced clarification. Shared cards, Cleanse, Blood Power and Winds can remove a specific binding; shared/Tactical transfers preserve a selected Bag's owner.
- `lokiTask` serialises Spellbound targeting and Bag distribution. Mandatory choices resolve before continuing the ability; optional distribution may finish early. Repeated recipients are legal, subject to caps. Antics II selects the three-Lies distribution option instead of its two-Lies gain. Vilify compares Health at activation, applying its upgraded Spellbound before distributing Bags. Kneel to Me and Tip the Scale trigger each “On” outcome once; explicit Scepter multiplication counts every matching die.
- Liar Liar! adds one damage per Scepter and resolves its Lies/Mischief Bag outcomes once each. Pantaloons on Fire! adds 2 alone or 4 if Liar Liar! is used on the same attack; either play order is supported. How Lovely! charges 1 CP plus the optional 2 extra. Take Backsies! selects a printed-cost-at-most-2 discard card and records its revealed name. Promo resolves Mischief or draws one on other outcomes.

Live browser layout, service-worker lifecycle and installed-PWA playtesting remain manual checks. Continue providing update ZIPs only unless the user asks for a complete build.

Scarlet Witch is appended as hero ID 29 in Marvel. Module: `docs/heroes/scarlet-witch.js`; artwork: `docs/art-hero-30.js`. - Reality Warp is Negative, cap 1. At actual Offensive Roll Phase entry, consume it and set serialisable `realityDie={seat,index:4}`. V2 Income prepares Main Phase 1 without consuming it, so it can still be removed before beginning the ORP. Knockdown defers actual entry until paid; skipping that ORP retains the token. `finishRoll` clears the borrowed die; extra ORPs and Déjà Vu start without it unless a new token is present.
- The borrowed die belongs to its roller for cards/statuses. Its number remains available for straights and numeric matching bonuses, but its symbol is excluded from activation and damage/reward counts for non-Scarlet heroes. Scarlet mirror matches retain symbols and may activate Ultimates. Symbol filtering applies only to the five offensive dice, not subsidiary rolls or defence. The UI uses Scarlet Witch's artwork and explicitly labels the unavailable symbol. `qualifies` accepts separate optional symbol dice; callers without a borrowed die retain prior behaviour.
- Probability Manipulation is Positive, cap 2. At instant timing after a roll, spend one to change an owned visible 2/4/6 by one; no increasing 6 or wrapping. Hex blanks are ineligible. Uses the actual current `rollContext`, including subsidiary/status rolls, foreign dice and mixed-owner contests. Announcement changes release the declaration. Ultimate subsidiary rolls remain locked. - Conjure is Positive, cap 1, once per global turn in own Main Phases. Choose Reality Warp for the opponent, or one Positive Status from either player's leaflet. Conjure is spent and its once-per-turn marker recorded before the block choice. The Reality Warp option has no discard block. Copying Bag of Tricks records the source Loki seat. Existing helpers handle Regenerate, Kinetic Energy and resource overflow correctly.
- Crackle is Positive, cap 3. On an activated non-pure attack, spend at most twice per turn. Count every remaining Positive/Negative token instance after removing the spent Crackle; add the count up to three as attack damage. Unique effects, companions and dials do not count. Remaining Crackle counts; both Regenerate sides count as their token instances. The supplied advanced clarification overrides the old leaflet's misleading Spellbound example. Can boost own Ultimate; cannot modify pure damage. Counts are evaluated at each spend, including after defence inflicts a status.
- Base Jinx resolves each On-symbol once across its whole ability: Warp adds two once; Chaos gains one Probability once; Spell inflicts Reality Warp once; Crown adds one and rerolls one die once. Thus maximum base damage is nine. Jinx II adds two per Warp and gains one Probability per Chaos; Spell remains an On effect; every Crown adds one and creates another reroll. No artificial repeat cap is imposed.
- `scarletJinx` queues Crown rerolls one at a time. Each new die is the sole active context once earlier dice have resolved. Cards cannot affect or match resolved original dice. Probability gained by earlier results can alter a new even result. Opponent responses occur before resolving each new die. Spell inflicts Reality Warp when its roll resolves, before any Crown reroll; prior-token metadata preserves defensive status prevention, and removal during a later response is not undone when the attack continues. The task and seen outcomes survive save/reload. Completion resumes through `scarletAbility` without applying the ability's initial gains twice.
- Mind Blast gains Crackle and rolls for five pure damage plus Probability on even, or eight pure on odd (nine upgraded). Hex blanks trigger neither parity result. Darkhold draws and gains its printed CP/tokens; both Spellcraft straight activators share one upgrade/binding while retaining distinct effects. Energy Drain steals up to three actual Health through the existing health-steal helper and does not activate defence or ordinary prevention.
- Double Trouble rolls five dice. Each pair threshold triggers once: two Warp gain one Crackle; two Chaos gain Probability; two Spell gain Conjure; two Crown return four damage. The upgrade grants two Crackle for at least three Warp and permits one pre-resolution Probability discard to set any defence die to any value. This is separate from ordinary even-number Probability spends.
- Warped! sets an owned current die to 1/2/3; No Dice! discards one Probability to set an opposing current die to 1; Chaos Manipulation! discards two to set any current die. Hex Shield! prevents one per Warp and inflicts Reality Warp on a Crown pair; it remains playable for that status effect after all attack damage has been prevented. Snap Crackle! gains one on even and two on odd. Hexellent! gains Conjure, Probability and Crackle on Crown, otherwise draws one.

## Spider-Man 0.37.0

Miles Morales: Spider-Man is appended as hero ID 30 in Marvel. Module: `docs/heroes/spider-man.js`; artwork: `docs/art-hero-31.js`. Uses the supplied Spider-Man archive and its V2 leaflet. Marvel shared artwork is automatic through set membership. Standard deck has four upgrades, ten unique action cards and eighteen shared cards; promo is optional.

Unique card IDs: 19 Punch II; 20 C-C-C-Combo II / Web Shot; 21 Ensnare II; 22 Venom Punch II / Combo Up; 23 Milkshake Me!; 24 Cha-Ching!; 25 Web Shooters!; 26 Radioactive Blood!; 27 Swing Escape!; 28 Ambush!; 29 Invisible Punch!; 30 Nice Try!; 31 Yikes!; 32 Booyah!; 33 optional Tingling!. No defence-upgrade cards are present. Base abilities include separate small/large Ensnare entries with a shared upgrade/binding.

- Combo is Unique, cap 1, removable and non-transferable. It is offered at `spiderCombo` only after a completed attack and survival of both players, including after any delayed Webbed damage. It can be spent once per global turn. `comboTurn` records the spend; gaining another token does not reset it. Spending starts another Offensive Roll Phase against the same opponent with no Income/Main Phase between them. Old Roll Phase statuses expire normally, and a newly inflicted Knockdown must be paid or the extra phase skipped. Declining keeps the token. Avoided attacks qualify; non-attack Web Shot does not. Opponent removal can interrupt the spend. Two-seat targeting inherently preserves the same opponent.
- Invisibility is Unique, cap 1, removable and non-transferable. Its holder may spend it after being targeted with undefendable or pure attack damage to open a defensive ability. It cannot respond to Ultimate, Stun, isolated or collateral-only damage, and Shadows still prevents defensive activation. `spiderDefenceUsed` enforces one actual defence per attack even if a second token is gained. Spending a token opens the defence choice; it does not grant two defensive activations.
- Counterpunch rolls three dice and returns one damage per Thwip. Spider-Sense rolls two dice and prevents half damage on any Spider, rounded up, once even for two Spiders. Normal flat-prevention/half-prevention ordering is retained. Spider-Sense may discard Invisibility for one additional selected-dice Roll Attempt before resolving. This is separate from Better D! and ordinary die alteration. A token spent to open the defence can be regained with Yikes! and then discarded for the extra attempt.
- Webbed is Negative, cap 1. `spiderMan.inflict()` adds a token only when below cap and queues a two-damage isolated source. `spiderWebHits` preserves source/recipient/lock metadata until the event can resolve without overwriting another side-damage event. Transfers count as a fresh infliction and deal the two damage on the new holder. Shared and Tactical transfers use this path; removing a token does not itself create damage. Ordinary generic status lists include Webbed for removal/prevention and Crackle counting.
- Existing Webbed converts the next normal attack to undefendable before defence, then is removed. It is consumed even when Shadows, Evasive or Parlay prevents the attack's damage. Pure, already-undefendable and Ultimate attacks do not consume it. An Instant removal opportunity remains before conversion in the targeting response window. Once conversion has resolved, removing another status does not undo the damage type.
- Ensnare stores `webAfter` and lists Webbed among its incoming statuses so Thick Skin II and Dryad can prevent it. The token is not inflicted during initial activation. After attack and return damage are settled, `finishRoll` inflicts the unblocked new token and its isolated two, then resumes via `spiderAfter` before normal Roll Phase cleanup/Combo choice. It does not change the already-resolved attack. If an older Webbed was consumed by this Ensnare, its replacement persists for a later attack. A prevented delayed token deals no isolated damage. Lethal attack/return damage ends the game without another opportunity.
- Venom Shockwave grants Invisibility and inflicts Webbed before its thirteen Ultimate damage. A newly gained token causes the two isolated damage, locked against opponent actions as part of the Ultimate. An existing Webbed stays at cap, causes no new isolated damage and is not consumed by the Ultimate. The two and thirteen are separate damage events. Web Shot grants Invisibility and inflicts Webbed without being an attack; the isolated damage alone does not permit Combo.
- Spider-Reflexes sums two subsidiary numerical dice and gains Combo at a total of five or less. Punch II uses four matching numbers for Combo, rather than counting four matching symbols.
